Milan based Lab & Independent Digital Label.

We are a playground for sonic evolution. Our mission is simple: to capture the universal frequency of inclusion through the only language that matters: Music.

We don't just sign artists; we seek emotional affinity and intellectual sincerity. We believe in the "human groove" and in the honest expression of the soul. Whether it’s a beat, a bassline, or a silence, if it’s sincere, it’s Aye Aye.

Creative sincerity. Artistic evolution. Digital heart.
